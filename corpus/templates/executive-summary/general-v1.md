# Executive Summary Template

## Purpose

Provide a concise overview that hooks investors and stakeholders in under two pages.

## Structure

1. **Company overview** — One paragraph: what you do, for whom, and why now.
2. **Problem** — The pain point, quantified where possible.
3. **Solution** — Your product/service and key differentiator.
4. **Market opportunity** — TAM/SAM/SOM with sources or reasonable assumptions.
5. **Business model** — How you make money (pricing, unit economics).
6. **Traction** — Milestones, pilots, LOIs, or projections for pre-revenue.
7. **Team** — Why this team wins.
8. **Financial highlights** — 3-year revenue summary, break-even timeline.
9. **Funding ask** — Amount, use of funds, expected runway (if applicable).

## Tone

- Confident, specific, no jargon without definition
- Lead with outcomes, not features
- Every claim should trace to a section in the full plan
