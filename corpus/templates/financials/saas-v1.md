# Financial Projections Template (SaaS)

### Cost structure

- **COGS:** hosting, payment processing, support per customer
- **OpEx:** engineering, sales & marketing, G&A
- **CAC** and **LTV** with LTV:CAC ratio target ≥ 3:1

### Key assumptions (document every number)

- Conversion rate (trial → paid)
- Monthly churn
- Average contract value
- Sales cycle length
- Headcount plan by quarter

## Narrative sections

1. **Assumptions** — Bullet list with rationale
2. **Revenue model** — Pricing tiers tied to value metrics
3. **Unit economics** — Contribution margin per customer
4. **Break-even analysis** — Month and conditions
5. **Funding requirements** — If raising, tie to milestones

## Red flags to avoid

- Hockey-stick with no driver
- Churn below industry without explanation
- CAC that ignores founder time or organic conflation
